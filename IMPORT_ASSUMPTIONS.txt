Confirmed import assumptions

1. Purchase price of $0 means Addie acquired the item for free.
2. COA means Certificate of Authenticity.
3. COA marked Y means a Certificate of Authenticity is included.
4. Blank listing price means the item has not been listed yet.
5. Items with no listing price and no listing platform are imported with status Purchased.
6. Items with a listing price or listing platform are imported with status Listed.
7. Items with a sale price are imported with status Sold.
